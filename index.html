About us

OUR STORY

In 1979, led by our unwavering commitment to wellness and longevity, we created an oasis of health. 100% VEGAN. 100% DELICIOUS. Founded in 1979, Soul Vegetarian was one of the first vegan restaurants in Atlanta. Soul Vegetarian Restaurants specializes in 100% vegan cuisine with the freshest ingredients daily. All of our delicious sweet treats are made with non-dairy ingredients. We also serve fresh juices and teas.

five star review by Benjamin R.:

This place has become a favorite for me and my wife. I normally get the kalebone (meat substitute) with 2 sides (mac n cheese and yams) and sweet cornbread. Now the mac n Cheese is so good, that I actually forgot that it was vegan cheese and not real cheese. It's good to know that you don't have to give up soul food in order to eat healthy. This place definitely lives up to its name.
